## How to Apply

SWU PHINMA uses an online application portal. Apply via:

**Application link:** https://bit.ly/COCApplyHere

This is the PHINMA Education application portal (shared across PHINMA network schools). You will need to:
1. Create an account
2. Fill out the online application form
3. Upload your cover letter, CV, and supporting documents
4. Select "Cebu City" as preferred location
5. Select "CAS Instructor" and "Philosophy" as specialization

1. **You are relocating to Cebu** — they want local candidates; you will be local
2. **Part-time flexibility** — matches their need and your MA study plans
3. **PRC license "is an advantage"** — note your UAE MoE license as equivalent professional credential
