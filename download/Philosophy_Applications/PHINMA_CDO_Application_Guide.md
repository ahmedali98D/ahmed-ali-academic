## Location Considerations

**Cagayan de Oro (CDO)** is in Northern Mindanao, Misamis Oriental province:
- 1-hour flight from Cebu (multiple daily flights)
- 1.5-hour ferry from Bohol via Camiguin route (slower but scenic)
- Lower cost of living than Cebu or Manila
- Major university city (multiple universities, including PHINMA CDO, Xavier University-Ateneo de Cagayan, MSU-IIT nearby)
- Safe, established expat community

**Carmen Campus** is the main PHINMA CDO campus, located in Carmen, Cagayan de Oro City.
