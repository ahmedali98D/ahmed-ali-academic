### 3. CSC Form 212 — Personal Data Sheet (Revised 2017) — ACTION REQUIRED FROM YOU

This is the Philippine Civil Service Commission's standard Personal Data Sheet form. It is a government form, NOT something I can generate for you — it must be filled out by you personally.

**Where to get it:**
- Download from the Philippine Civil Service Commission website: https://csc.gov.ph
- Direct download link (when available): search "CSC Form 212 Revised 2017" at csc.gov.ph/poster
- Alternative: any Philippine university HR office can provide a copy

**How to fill it out:**
- Print on legal-size paper (8.5" × 14")
- Use black ink, print legibly
- Fill in ALL fields — incomplete forms may be rejected
- For fields that don't apply, write "N/A" (not "—")
- Sign each page in the designated areas
- Have it notarized if requested (some universities require this)

**Sections requiring your attention:**
- Personal information (name, date of birth, place of birth, citizenship, etc.)
- Family background
- Educational background (list BA Philosophy and PGDE with attested TORs)
- Eligibility (you can note your UAE MoE Teaching License and IELTS Band 7 here)
- Work experience (list all roles from your CV)
- Voluntary work / civic organizations (BPA, Bibliotheca Alexandrina youth club)
- Training programs (your certifications)
- Skills (languages, technical)
- References (use Dr. Rahimjon Abdugafurov and two others)
- Character references

## Important Notes

1. **The "Through Proper Channels" routing** means the application letter goes through the Department Chairperson (Reynante B. Casiro) before reaching the University President. Address the letter to Dr. Eballe but submit to Mr. Casiro — this is standard Philippine academic protocol.

2. **Contract of Service (COS)** means this is not a permanent plantilla position. It is a semester-by-semester contract. This is normal for first-time hires and is a common pathway to a permanent role.

3. **Reference to "Ustadh" before Dr. Abdugafurov's title** in the application letter — "Ustadh" is the Arabic honorific for "teacher/scholar." It signals cultural respect and may be recognized by Dr. Eballe (a PhD philosopher). If you prefer to omit it, simply remove "Ustadh" from the letter.

4. **Your IELTS Band 7** satisfies English proficiency requirements. Your UAE MoE Teaching License satisfies the "Licensed board passer is an advantage" qualification — note that Philippine PRC license is different, but your UAE license is an equivalent professional credential.

5. **The book is your strongest credential.** If possible, include a physical copy with the courier package, or at minimum include clear photos of the front cover, title page, and ISBN page in your supporting documents PDF.
